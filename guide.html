Pathway: admin guide (one page)

For church admins and volunteers. All day-to-day settings are changed in the church Google Sheet, not in code.

Who does what

  • Admin: sees the whole church, dashboard and admin page.
  • Leader: their campus; approves requests, builds the rota.
  • Follow-up worker: only the new converts given to them.
  • Counsellor: captures new converts and shows QR codes.
  • Member: My space, requests and trips.

Add a person who signs in

  1. Open the Sheet, tab Workers. Add a row: name, role, campus, Active = Yes.
  2. Type a 4-digit PIN in the New PIN column.
  3. Click Pathway > Apply new PINs. The PIN is hidden and saved.
  4. Tell them their name (exactly as typed) and PIN privately.
  5. Optional: add their email so they get reminders by email.

Forgotten PIN, locked out, or leaving

  • Forgotten / locked: type a new PIN in Workers, then Pathway > Apply new PINs.
  • Leaving: set Active to No. They are signed out everywhere.
  • Members who only use My space still need a row in Workers with role Member.

Change church settings

  • Campuses, Events (each event gets its own QR code), Groups, Departments: add a row, Active = Yes. To retire one, set Active = No.
  • Stages: rename a stage or edit its "next step" hint.
  • Settings: contact deadline, stall days, assignment rule, rota day, email on/off.
  • Approval Routes: who approves each request type, in order (up to 4 steps).
  • Approvers: who "Senior pastor", "Finance" and similar titles are. Blank means any Admin.
  • After a change, tap Refresh in the app, or close and reopen it.

Golden rules

  • Never rename or delete a tab or a column heading. Add rows only.
  • Names must match exactly everywhere. The leader of a group and the head of a department must be spelt as in Workers, or they will not get approvals.
  • Do not edit the Audit tab, or the hidden PIN and session columns.
  • Prayer notes are for the pastoral team only. Never share PINs.

Every week

  • Dashboard: check "Stalled / unassigned". Give new converts a follow-up worker.
  • Permissions: clear anything waiting for you.
  • Stewards: check the Sunday rota on Thursday or Friday and fill any red gaps.
  • Remind group leaders to log their weekly meeting.

Every month

  • Backup: in the Sheet, File > Download > Microsoft Excel. Keep the file safely.
  • Review stalled people with the follow-up team.
  • Deletion requests: open the person in Converts, then use Delete personal details at the bottom of their profile (Admin only).

One-time switches

  • Pathway > Turn on daily reminders & rota: morning follow-up reminders, automatic Sunday rota, trips closed when people return.
  • Pathway > Build rota for next Sunday: build the rota by hand at any time.

If something goes wrong

  • Strange error, or a column missing: click Pathway > Set up / repair tabs. It never deletes data.
  • "Not connected to the church Sheet": the web address in config.js is missing or wrong. Ask the person who set up Pathway.
  • New screens not showing: close the app fully and open it again.
  • Phone offline: captures are saved on the phone and send by themselves later.
