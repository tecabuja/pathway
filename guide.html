Pathway: admin guide (one page)

For church admins and leaders. Everything is done in the Pathway app. Nobody needs to open the Google Sheet.

Who does what

  • Admin: the whole church, and everything in Admin & settings.
  • Leader: their campus. Adds follow-up workers, counsellors and members; manages events, small groups and departments; approves requests and builds the rota.
  • Follow-up worker: only the new converts given to them.
  • Counsellor: captures new converts and shows QR codes.
  • Member: My space, service grids, requests and trips.
  • Service grid builders: Admins, plus anyone with "Can build service grids" = Yes.

Let someone sign in

  1. Go to Admin & settings > Workers & PINs > + Add.
  2. Type their name exactly as people know them, then choose a role and campus.
  3. Give a 4-digit PIN, then Save.
  4. Tell them their name and PIN privately. They can change the PIN in My space.

Forgotten PIN, locked out, or leaving

  • Forgotten or locked: open the worker and type a new PIN. This also unlocks them.
  • Leaving: open the worker and tap Remove. They are signed out everywhere and their history is kept. To bring them back, set Active to Yes.
  • Renaming someone updates them everywhere: groups, departments, approvals and service grids. Give them a new PIN after a rename.

Change church set-up

  • Campuses, Events & QR, Small groups, Departments: tap + Add, or tap a row to edit or remove it. Renames carry through everywhere.
  • Pathway stages: rename stages and edit the "next step" hint.
  • Approvals: who approves each request type, in order, and who titles like "Senior pastor" are.
  • Bulk upload: add many records at once. Choose the list, tap Download template, fill it in Excel (one row per record), save as CSV and upload it, or copy the rows from Excel and paste them. Existing records are updated, never doubled. Rows with a problem are listed and can be downloaded, fixed and uploaded again.
  • Rules: contact deadline, stall days, assignment rule, rota day, emails, reminder time, venue and more. Campus word and Campus word (plural) change what the app calls a campus (for example Branch / Branches).
  • Members screen: + Add member, edit, or remove from the directory.
  • Convert profile: Edit details fixes a name, phone or other details.

Golden rules

  • Never share PINs. Each person has their own.
  • Prayer notes are for the pastoral team only.
  • Remove people who leave a role the same week.
  • Keep at least two admins who can sign in.

Every week

  • Dashboard: check "Stalled / unassigned" and give new converts a follow-up worker.
  • Permissions: clear anything waiting for you.
  • Stewards: check the Sunday rota on Thursday or Friday and fill any red gaps.
  • Service grids: publish by midweek and check who has confirmed.

Polls, forms and RSVPs

  • Polls & forms > + New poll or form, pick a starter (meeting date, RSVP, feedback, volunteer sign-up, prayer request), change the questions, then Create and share.
  • Share the link on WhatsApp or show the QR code. Anyone can answer without signing in. Choose Send to workers or members to see who has not answered and remind them.
  • Results shows live charts, the full list of answers and Download (Excel). A closing date or a number of places closes it by itself.

Messages

  • Messages > + New message: choose people from Pathway, or upload or paste a list (Name, Phone, Email). Type the message with {first} where the name goes.
  • WhatsApp / SMS: tap the green button for each person; it opens WhatsApp with their own name filled in. Press Send, come back, tap the next one. It is free and goes from your own number.
  • Email goes by itself from the church Gmail (about 100 a day).
  • Automatic tab: birthday wishes, event-day reminders, weekly reminders and a welcome for new converts. They run each morning when daily reminders are on. Add each member's birthday day and month (and email) in Members.

Church calendar and What's on page

  • Church calendar > + Add event: name, kind, date, time, venue. Tick Public to show it on the What's on page (events.html) that anyone can open without signing in. Untick for church-only meetings.
  • Choose an RSVP from Polls & forms so people can register. Published service grids show on the calendar by themselves.
  • Share the What's on page link in WhatsApp groups or on social media. It shows a countdown to the next event, directions and "Add to my calendar".

Leaders, grids and stewards

  • In Workers & PINs, set each leader's Group or department. Leaders then see only their own people (Rules > What Leaders can see). Their Admin menu becomes Team & PINs and Bulk upload for their own team only.
  • Only people with Can build service grids = Yes can create grids, Admins included. Everyone signed in can view published grids.
  • A steward is a member with a department: Stewards > + Add steward, or set the Department in Members or in the Members bulk-upload file.
  • Approved Travel or Leave from duty requests go onto the Away log by themselves.

Every month

  • Export & backup > Download full backup (Excel). Keep the file safely.
  • Dashboard > Print report for the pastorate meeting.
  • Deletion requests: open the convert and use Delete personal details (Admin only).

Switches

  • Overview > Daily reminders & automatic rota: turn on or off.
  • Overview > Check & repair: if something looks wrong after an update. It never deletes data.
  • Audit trail: see who changed what, and when.

If something goes wrong

  • "Not connected to the church server": the app's server address is missing. Ask the person who installed Pathway.
  • New screens not showing: close the app fully and open it again.
  • Phone offline: captures are saved on the phone and send by themselves later.
